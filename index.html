Bangladesh BTRC IMEI & Seller Verification

        

          

            1. Dual IMEI Match: Dial *#06# and match the 15-digit IMEI with box and SIM tray.
          

          

            2. BTRC / NEIR SMS Check: Send KYD <15-digit IMEI> to 16002 (or dial *16161#) before paying.
          

          

            3. Seller Proof Log: Prefer box/memo sets. For boxless phones, take seller's NID photo, phone number & signed handover slip.
          

          

            4. Public Meetup Rule: Always meet at busy public spots (mall gate, market entrance, or campus) — never isolated alleys.
